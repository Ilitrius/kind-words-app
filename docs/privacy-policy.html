Privacy Policy

App: Kind Words (package: app.rpkindwords) · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Summary
  • Your sentences, favorites and settings stay on your device. The developer runs no server that receives data from you and receives no data from you.
  • At most once a day, in the background, the app checks whether there are new sentences and, if so, downloads them from a database at Supabase (data center in the EU). This is read-only; no data about you is sent, except that your IP address and the time of the request technically arise (section 5c). The app works fully without this retrieval.
  • The app shows ads (one banner on the home screen and on favorites) from Google AdMob. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland the app first asks for your consent; without it, ads do not start. You can change your choice at any time in the settings under "Privacy options for ads" (section 5b).
  • With ads, Google processes, among other things, your advertising ID, your IP address or approximate location, device information and ad interactions, also in the USA. The optional translation with Google ML Kit starts only after a separate consent (section 12d).
  • The app has no user account and no analytics or tracking services of its own. An optional donate button only opens an external link in your browser (section 11).
  • You can delete your data in the app at any time or remove it by uninstalling.

2. Purpose of the app

Kind Words suggests kind words and short affectionate sentences that you can say to your partner or send through a messenger. If you wish, the app reminds you during the day.

3. Data processed

  • Favorites and hidden sentences that you mark
  • Sentences you write yourself
  • App settings: reminder mode, times, weekdays, content language

The bundled sentences are part of the app and contain no personal data; the app can download new sentences when needed (section 5c). So that suggestions match your tone, the app analyses your own sentences locally on the device only (for example length, ending, tone). The result is neither stored nor transmitted. Your own sentences may contain whatever you type; they are processed locally only.

4. Storage on the device only

All data is stored locally in the app's protected storage on your device (local database and app settings). There is no developer server that receives data from you, no user account and no cloud sync. The app uses the internet permission for ads (section 5b), retrieving new sentences (section 5c) and the optional translation (section 12d); it does not transmit your sentences, favorites or settings. Android Auto Backup is disabled for the app.

Backup (export/import): In the settings you can save your own sentences, favorites and settings as a JSON file to a location you choose, and restore them from such a file. The file is stored only where you decide; the app does not send it and only reads files you select yourself. Importing only adds data and deletes nothing.

Permissions: when you enable reminders, the app asks for the notifications permission (Android 13 and later). It also uses the permission to be started after the device restarts (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) so that reminders can be scheduled again after a reboot, and Android's built-in alarm service, which needs no special permission. These serve local scheduling only; no data is transmitted. The home screen widget (section 12e) uses a library (Android WorkManager) that adds the permissions WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE; they only let Android update the widget reliably, and the app does not run a foreground service of its own.

Update notice: if the app was installed through Google Play, it asks the Google Play Store app on your device (Play Core / In-App Updates) at start whether a newer version exists and, if so, shows a hint. This query is handled by the Play Store app on your device under Google's own terms; Kind Words sends no data of its own and receives only the information that an update is available.

5. Disclosure to third parties

The app contains no crash-reporting services and no analytics or tracking services of its own. Third parties are involved only for ads (Google AdMob, section 5b), for retrieving new sentences (Supabase as the developer's service provider, section 5c) and the optional translation (Google ML Kit, section 12d, only after consent). The developer sells no data, and the app does not pass on your sentences, favorites or settings.

5a. Update notice via Google Play

At start, at most once a day, the app asks the Google Play services whether a new version is available and then shows a notice. The query is handled by the Google Play Store on your device; the app itself transmits no data and only learns that an update is available. Google's privacy policy applies to the processing by Google Play. If the app was not installed through Google Play, no notice appears.

5b. Ads (Google AdMob) and consent

What is shown: The app shows an ad banner at the bottom of the home screen and the favorites screen. There are no ads on the other screens, in the introduction or when sharing, and no full-screen or start-up ads. The provider is Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA). The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not a children's app.

What data does Google process? To show, measure and bill ads and to detect abuse and fraud, Google may process through the ads SDK in the app in particular: your device's advertising ID, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (such as country or region, no precise location), device and app information (manufacturer, model, Android version, language, package name, app version, screen size), information about ad interactions (ad loaded, shown, tapped) and diagnostics and performance data (such as load times, errors). With your consent, ads can be personalized using this data, and Google and advertising partners may store and read information on your device for this. The app does not transmit any of your sentences, favorites or settings in doing so. The developer receives only aggregated reports from Google (such as the number of impressions and revenue), no data about individual users.

Consent (EU, UK, Switzerland): In these regions the app shows a consent request at start from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP, a consent management system provided by Google). Only when you agree there (or have made your choice and ads are then permitted) is the ads SDK started and an ad requested. If you decline, no ads are requested in these regions; all features of the app remain usable. To find out whether a request is needed and what you chose earlier, the app queries your consent status from Google at every start; technical data such as your IP address is transmitted in doing so. In other regions the app shows no consent request of its own; Google's terms for advertising apply there.

Legal basis: Your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where information on your device is accessed (such as the advertising ID), Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG. Querying the consent status at start serves to document and comply with the consent requirement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Withdrawal: You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: in the app under Settings → "Privacy options for ads" (this entry appears where a consent request is required). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it. In addition, in the Android settings (Privacy or Google → Ads) you can reset or delete the advertising ID and turn off personalized ads, and you can manage your ad settings at Google via myadcenter.google.com.

Recipients and third countries: The recipient of the data is Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC). According to Google, it is responsible for its own processing to deliver ads; data may also be transferred to the USA and other third countries. According to Google, this relies on adequacy decisions (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses. See policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Retention: The developer itself stores no personal data for ads; only your consent choice is remembered locally on your device by the consent system until you change it or clear the app data. For retention at Google, Google's statements apply (see the links above).

Not required: Consent is voluntary. The app can be used fully without ad consent; no ads are then shown. Under the GDPR, consent is effective on its own only from age 16 (for younger people only with the consent of the person with parental responsibility); please give it only if you are allowed to consent under the law of your country.

5c. Downloading new sentences (Supabase)

What happens: When the app starts, at most once a day and in the background, it asks a database of the developer whether a newer version of the sentences exists for your content language. If so, it downloads the sentences and merges them with the ones on your device. Your favorites, hidden sentences and own sentences are kept. If the retrieval fails (for example without a network), the app carries on unchanged with the existing and bundled sentences and tries again later. The app also works fully without this retrieval. The retrieval is not for advertising and is independent of your choice on ad consent.

What data is transmitted? The app only reads public content (a version number and sentence texts). It sends no user data: no sentences, favorites or settings, no advertising ID, no device or installation identifier, and there is no user account. As with any internet connection, your IP address, the time of the request and the usual connection data (for example the requested path and the identifier of Android's standard network library) technically arise at the provider. The developer does not evaluate this data and does not combine it with other data; the provider may log it briefly to operate and secure its infrastructure.

Provider and recipients: The developer runs the database at Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA) in the EU region (Ireland, Amazon Web Services data center). Supabase processes the data as a service provider on behalf of the developer (processing under Art. 28 GDPR). The app's access is read-only and anonymous; write access is blocked in the database.

Third countries: The data is hosted in the EU. Because the provider Supabase, Inc. is a US company (and upstream infrastructure such as a content delivery network may be involved), access to connection data from a third country cannot be ruled out entirely. The safeguard is the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses in the provider's data processing agreement. Purpose and legal basis: The purpose is to keep the app's sentences up to date and to extend them without you having to install an app update. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in a working and current offering (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); your interests are protected because no user data is transmitted and the IP address arises only technically. No information on your device is stored or read for advertising or analytics in doing so; locally the app only remembers the version number of the sentences and the time of the last check.

Retention and objection: The developer stores no data from the retrieval. For short-term technical logs at the provider, its statements apply (supabase.com/privacy). You can object to the retrieval (Art. 21 GDPR) by revoking the app's internet access or background data in the Android settings; the app currently has no setting of its own for this.

6. Sharing and copying (user-controlled)

When you share a sentence, the app opens the Android share sheet. You choose which app (for example a messenger) receives the text; from then on, that app's privacy terms apply. Copying places the sentence on your device's clipboard.

7. Legal basis

Your sentences, favorites and settings stay exclusively on your device; the developer has no access to them. Insofar as processing on your device is legally relevant, it takes place at your initiative (Art. 6(1)(a) or (b) GDPR). For ads (section 5b) and the optional translation (section 12d) the legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (and Section 25(1) TDDDG); Google processes the data in doing so. For retrieving new sentences (section 5c) it is the legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

8. Retention and deletion

Data remains stored until you delete it. You can delete your own sentences individually and remove favorites at any time. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data in Android settings (Apps → Kind Words → Storage → Clear data) removes all locally stored data.

9. Your rights

Because the developer receives no data from you, there is no data to disclose. You are in full control and can view and delete everything yourself in the app. For the ad data processed by Google, direct your requests to Google (see section 5b). When new sentences are retrieved (section 5c), the developer stores no data about you; direct requests about it to the developer (section 13). Irrespective of this, under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, the right to withdraw a given consent at any time (section 5b), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the place of your habitual residence, your workplace or the place of the alleged infringement. The authority responsible for the developer is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

10. Children and teens

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It contains no mature content and no communication with other users. The app shows ads from Google AdMob (section 5b); it is not a children's app, and requests to Google are made without any age setting. Under the GDPR, consent to ads is effective on its own only from age 16. Parents can prevent ads by declining the consent request.

11. Voluntary donation (external link)

The app's settings include an optional donate button. It opens a link in your browser. The app transmits no data in doing so and learns neither of your payment nor of your name. The donation is handled entirely by an external payment provider whose privacy policy applies. Donating is voluntary, unlocks no features and is not required.

12. Changes

If the app's data processing changes (for example through new features), this policy will be updated before the corresponding app version is released. The version published here applies.

12a. Rating and feedback

In the settings there are the buttons "Rate the app" and "Send feedback". "Rate the app" only opens your Play Store app (or, as a fallback, your browser) on this app's page; the app transmits no data. "Send feedback" opens your email app with an empty message to the contact address and the version number in the subject. You alone decide whether and what to send; the message leaves your device only when you send it from your email app. There is no automatic rating prompt.

12b. Learning on your device

To make suggestions fit you better, the app remembers locally which sentences you share, copy, save as favorites or skip, and analyzes the style of your own sentences. This only produces weights for choosing suggestions. The data stays on your device, is not transmitted, not used for advertising and not written to the backup file. It is removed when you clear the app data or uninstall. No external AI model is trained.

12c. Suggest a sentence (voluntary)

For your own sentences there is a "Suggest this sentence" button. It opens your email app with the sentence as a draft to the contact address. Only if you tap "Send" there yourself does the sentence reach us (together with your email address as sender). The app sends nothing on its own. We use submitted sentences only to possibly add them, reworded and without any link to you, to the app; please send no names or private details. The legal basis is your voluntary submission (Art. 6(1)(a)/(b) GDPR); on request we delete your email.

12d. Reading aloud and translation (optional)

Reading aloud: For the sentences that come with the app, the app plays natural-sounding voice recordings that are part of the app package (synthetic voice, generated in advance by the developer with a text-to-speech service; no recording of a real person and no download of the recordings from the internet while you use the app). Your own sentences, emoji and text symbols and translated sentences are read with your device's text-to-speech instead; only locally installed voices are used and network voices are excluded. The app stores tempo and chosen voice locally. Nothing is transmitted.

Translation: This feature is off by default. When you turn it on, we ask for your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Only then is Google ML Kit (on-device translation, Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) started. Your sentences are translated on the device and not sent. For translation to work, the app downloads language models from the internet on your explicit action (about 30 MB per language, Wi-Fi only by default); for this the app holds the permissions INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. According to Google, the ML Kit SDK may then send technical diagnostics and usage-analytics data to Google: device information (manufacturer, model, Android version), app information (package name, version), a per-installation identifier, performance metrics, the chosen languages, events such as model downloads and error codes. Google encrypts this transfer (HTTPS) and states it does not pass the data on to third parties; Google's privacy policy and the ML Kit data disclosure apply (developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). Without your consent the SDK is not started.

Withdrawal: In the settings you can turn translation off at any time. The SDK is then no longer used, and the app can delete the downloaded language models on request. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it. The app stores your consent locally with a timestamp and the version of the consent text.

12e. Home screen widget (optional)

You can add the "Kind word of the day" widget to your home screen. It shows one sentence from the app's own sentences, your own sentences or your favorites, in the content language you use in the app, and it offers the buttons "New" and "Share". For each widget the app stores locally which category you chose and which sentence is currently shown (in a separate local settings file, removed when you delete the widget or clear the app data). The widget changes its sentence once a day and when you tap "New". Nothing is transmitted and no advertising is shown. "Share" opens the Android share sheet as described in section 6. The widget needs no permission of its own.
