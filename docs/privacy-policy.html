Privacy Policy

App: Kind Words (package: app.rpkindwords) · Last updated: 27 October 2026

Summary
  • The app does not collect, transmit or share any data. Everything stays on your device.
  • The app has no internet permission of its own (the update notice runs through Google Play, see section 5a), no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no user account. An optional donate button only opens an external link in your browser (see section 11).
  • You can delete your data in the app at any time or remove it by uninstalling.

2. Purpose of the app

Kind Words suggests kind words and short affectionate sentences that you can say to your partner or send through a messenger. If you wish, the app reminds you during the day.

3. Data processed

  • Favorites and hidden sentences that you mark
  • Sentences you write yourself
  • App settings: reminder mode, times, weekdays, content language

The bundled sentences are part of the app and contain no personal data. So that suggestions match your tone, the app analyses your own sentences locally on the device only (for example length, ending, tone). The result is neither stored nor transmitted. Your own sentences may contain whatever you type; they are processed locally only.

4. Storage on the device only

All data is stored locally in the app's protected storage on your device (local database and app settings). There are no developer servers, no user account and no cloud sync. The app has no internet permission of its own, so data cannot leave the app on its own. Android Auto Backup is disabled for the app.

Backup (export/import): In the settings you can save your own sentences, favorites and settings as a JSON file to a location you choose, and restore them from such a file. The file is stored only where you decide; the app does not send it and only reads files you select yourself. Importing only adds data and deletes nothing.

Permissions: when you enable reminders, the app asks for the notifications permission (Android 13 and later). It also uses the permission to be started after the device restarts (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) so that reminders can be scheduled again after a reboot, and Android's built-in alarm service, which needs no special permission. These serve local scheduling only; no data is transmitted.

5. No sharing, no third parties

The app contains no ads, no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services and no third-party data-collection SDKs. No data is shared, sold or transferred to third parties or third countries.

5a. Update notice via Google Play

At start, at most once a day, the app asks the Google Play services whether a new version is available and then shows a notice. The query is handled by the Google Play Store on your device; the app itself transmits no data and only learns that an update is available. Google's privacy policy applies to the processing by Google Play. If the app was not installed through Google Play, no notice appears.

6. Sharing and copying (user-controlled)

When you share a sentence, the app opens the Android share sheet. You choose which app (for example a messenger) receives the text; from then on, that app's privacy terms apply. Copying places the sentence on your device's clipboard.

7. Legal basis

Because data stays exclusively on your device and the developer has no access to it, the developer does not process personal data within the meaning of the GDPR. Insofar as processing on your device is legally relevant, it takes place at your initiative (Art. 6(1)(a) or (b) GDPR).

8. Retention and deletion

Data remains stored until you delete it. You can delete your own sentences individually and remove favorites at any time. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data in Android settings (Apps → Kind Words → Storage → Clear data) removes all locally stored data.

9. Your rights

Because the developer receives no data from you, there is no data to disclose. You are in full control and can view and delete everything yourself in the app. Irrespective of this, under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Children and teens

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It contains no mature content, no ads, no communication with other users and collects no data.

11. Voluntary donation (external link)

The app's settings include an optional donate button. It opens a link in your browser. The app transmits no data in doing so and learns neither of your payment nor of your name. The donation is handled entirely by an external payment provider whose privacy policy applies. Donating is voluntary, unlocks no features and is not required.

12. Changes

If the app's data processing changes (for example through new features), this policy will be updated before the corresponding app version is released. The version published here applies.
