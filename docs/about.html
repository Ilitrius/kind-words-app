About RP Kind Words

Small kind words make a big difference. Kind Words suggests loving sentences you can say to your partner or send in a message, and gently reminds you to do it.

What the app does

  • Kind sentences in 10 categories, such as love, appreciation, good morning, good night and humor
  • Share to any messenger in one tap, or copy
  • Save favorites and write your own sentences
  • Reminders at random times or at fixed times
  • German and English

Privacy

Everything stays on your device. No ads, no tracking, no account. More in the privacy policy.
