About RP Kind Words

Small kind words make a big difference. Kind Words suggests loving sentences you can say to your partner or send in a message, and gently reminds you to do it.

What the app does

  • Kind sentences in 12 categories, such as love, appreciation, good morning, good night and humor
  • Share to any messenger in one tap, or copy
  • Save favorites and write your own sentences
  • Reminders at random times or at fixed times
  • German and English

Privacy

Your sentences, favorites and settings stay on your device, and you don't need an account. The app downloads new sentences at most once a day in the background, without sending any data about you; everything also works without it.

Ads

The app is free and shows an ad banner from Google AdMob in return, only on the home screen and on favorites. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first; if you say no, you see no ads and can still use everything. You can change your choice at any time in the settings under "Privacy options for ads". For ads, Google processes for example your advertising ID, your IP address or approximate location, device information and ad interactions, also in the USA. All details are in the privacy policy.
