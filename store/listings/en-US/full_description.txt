A kind word costs nothing and often means a lot. But the right words don't always come to mind. Kind Words suggests short, warm sentences for your partner. You send them or say them out loud, and the app nudges you now and then.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Around 290 sentences in 12 categories: love, appreciation, good morning, good night, encouragement, missing you, compliments, humor, thank you, sorry, emoji and text symbols
• Share to any messenger in one tap, or copy
• Save favorites, and they get suggested more often
• Write, edit and keep your own sentences. Suggestions slowly adapt to your style
• Reminders at fixed times, or at random within a window, on the days you pick
• Back up your sentences, favorites and settings to a file, for example for a new phone
• In English and German, switchable in the settings
• Light and dark mode

PRIVACY
• Your sentences and settings stay on your device
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• The app has no internet permission
• You'll find the privacy policy in the settings

Kind Words is free. If you like it, you can donate in the settings. You don't have to, and you get nothing extra for it.
