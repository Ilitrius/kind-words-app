Small kind words make a big difference. Kind Words gives you loving sentences to say to your partner or send in a message, and gently reminds you to do it.

WHAT YOU GET
• Hundreds of kind sentences in 10 categories: love, appreciation, good morning, good night, encouragement, missing you, compliments, humor, thank you and sorry
• Share to any messenger in one tap, or copy to the clipboard
• Save your favorites
• Write, edit and keep your own sentences
• Reminders: at random times within a window you choose, or at fixed times on the days you pick
• German and English

YOUR PRIVACY
• Everything stays on your device
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• The app does not even have internet access

Kind Words is free. If you like it, you can make a voluntary donation in the settings. It unlocks nothing and is never required.
