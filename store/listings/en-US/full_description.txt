A kind word costs nothing and often means a lot. But the right words don't always come to mind. Kind Words suggests short, warm sentences for your partner: love messages, sweet texts and compliments for every day. You send them by WhatsApp or SMS or say them out loud, and the app nudges you now and then.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap "New suggestion" and read the sentence.
2. Share it in one tap to any messenger or by SMS, or copy it.
3. If you like it, save it as a favorite. Favorites are suggested more often.

WHEN IT HELPS
• For good morning texts and good night messages when you wake up or fall asleep apart
• For a compliment, a thank you, an "I miss you" or a quick "thinking of you"
• When you want to cheer someone up or say sorry and the words won't come
• In a long-distance relationship, to keep your girlfriend or boyfriend close to your everyday life
• For anniversaries, small surprises or just because, as a sweet love note

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Around 1250 sentences in 12 categories: love, appreciation, good morning, good night, encouragement, missing you, compliments, humor, thank you, sorry, emoji and text symbols
• Share to any messenger in one tap, or copy
• Save favorites, and they get suggested more often
• Write, edit and keep your own sentences. Suggestions slowly adapt to your style
• A home screen widget with a kind word of the day, new every day, with your choice of category, plus "New" and "Share" buttons
• Reminders at fixed times, or at random within a window, on the days you pick
• Back up your sentences, favorites and settings to a file, for example for a new phone
• A short introduction at first start that you can skip
• In English and German, switchable in the settings
• Read sentences aloud, slowly and warmly, with a natural-sounding voice and tempo you choose, optionally translated first into one of 12 languages (for example German to Italian)
• Light and dark mode

FOR EVERY MOMENT
Romantic quotes, declarations of love and compliments for her and for him. Warm words for new couples, engaged and married partners, and those who have been together for years. Plus words to cheer someone up or comfort them, an honest sorry or a heartfelt thank you. Fitting texts for Valentine's Day, wedding anniversaries and birthdays, little love notes, relationship moments in daily life, and emojis or text symbols to send.

PRIVACY
• Your sentences, favorites and settings stay on your device
• No account needed
• The app contains ads: one small banner from Google AdMob, only on the home screen and on favorites. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your permission first, and you can change it any time in the settings under "Privacy options for ads". If you say no, you see no ads
• For ads, Google processes for example the advertising ID, approximate location, device info and ad interactions
• Reading aloud works without internet: the natural voice recordings come with the app (synthetic voice), your own sentences use your device's text-to-speech
• The widget works on the device only and shows no ads
• Translation is optional and off by default. Only after your consent does the app use Google ML Kit: sentences are translated on the device, the app downloads the language models once from the internet, and Google may receive technical diagnostics data while doing so
• The app never sends your sentences or favorites; notices about new versions come through Google Play
• Rating, donating and feedback open the Play Store, your browser or your mail app, only when you tap them
• You'll find the privacy policy in the settings

Kind Words is free and paid for by the small ad banner. If you like it, you can rate the app or donate in the settings. Ideas or problems? Write to us with "Send feedback".
