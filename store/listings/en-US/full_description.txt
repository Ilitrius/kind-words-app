A kind word costs nothing and often means a lot. Kind Words gives you short sentences for your partner. You send them or say them out loud, and the app nudges you now and then.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Around 200 sentences in 10 categories: love, appreciation, good morning, good night, encouragement, missing you, compliments, humor, thank you and sorry
• Share to any messenger in one tap, or copy
• Save your favorites
• Write, edit and keep your own sentences
• Reminders at fixed times, or at random within a window, on the days you pick
• Back up your sentences and favorites to a file
• In English and German

PRIVACY
• Everything stays on your device
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• The app has no internet permission

Kind Words is free. If you like it, you can donate in the settings. You don't have to, and you get nothing extra for it.
