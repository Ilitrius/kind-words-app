A kind word costs nothing and often means a lot. But the right words don't always come to mind. Kind Words suggests short, warm sentences for your partner. You send them in a message or say them out loud, and the app nudges you now and then.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap "New suggestion" and read the sentence.
2. Share it in one tap to any messenger or by SMS, or copy it.
3. If you like it, save it as a favorite. Favorites are suggested more often.

WHEN IT HELPS
• For a good morning or good night when you wake up or fall asleep apart
• For a compliment, a thank you or a quick "thinking of you"
• When you want to cheer someone up or say sorry and the words won't come
• In a long-distance relationship, to share the everyday moments anyway
• For anniversaries, small surprises or just because

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Around 570 sentences in 12 categories: love, appreciation, good morning, good night, encouragement, missing you, compliments, humor, thank you, sorry, emoji and text symbols
• Share to any messenger in one tap, or copy
• Save favorites, and they get suggested more often
• Write, edit and keep your own sentences. Suggestions slowly adapt to your style
• Reminders at fixed times, or at random within a window, on the days you pick
• Back up your sentences, favorites and settings to a file, for example for a new phone
• A short introduction at first start that you can skip
• In English and German, switchable in the settings
• Read sentences aloud, slowly and warmly, with a voice and tempo you choose, optionally translated first into one of 12 languages (for example German to Italian)
• Light and dark mode

PRIVACY
• Your sentences, favorites and settings stay on your device
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Reading aloud uses your device's own text-to-speech, without internet
• Translation is optional and off by default. Only after your consent does the app use Google ML Kit: sentences are translated on the device, the app downloads the language models once from the internet, and Google may receive technical diagnostics data while doing so
• Without translation the app sends no data of its own; notices about new versions come through Google Play
• Rating, donating and feedback open the Play Store, your browser or your mail app, only when you tap them
• You'll find the privacy policy in the settings

Kind Words is free. If you like it, you can rate the app or donate in the settings. You don't have to, and you get nothing extra for it. Ideas or problems? Write to us with "Send feedback".
